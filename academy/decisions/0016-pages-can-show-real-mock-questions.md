## Question

Some stories and diagrams hold real questions of mocks 1 to 5 with the correct answer. Examples: mock decision 0074 gives the answer of question M5-S38, and diagram 11 shows two real questions and one failed try that never went into a mock, each with the correct option marked. The brief says that a participant sees the link to a mock question only after they sit that mock. Can a page show a real mock question with its answer?

## Options

1. No. A story describes the question in general terms, or uses a failed try that never went into a mock.
2. Yes, where the story needs it.

## Decision

Option 2. A page can show a real mock question with its answer, where the story needs it.

## Reason

The owner said that one or two questions on a page are not a problem, because the repository becomes public with the mock answers (decision 0003). The participants are adults. A participant who uses the answers to cheat on a mock will still not pass the real exam.

## How we decided

The independent review of the plan found that tab 2 and the redrawn diagrams would show real questions with their answers. The assistant recommended option 1. The owner chose option 2.
