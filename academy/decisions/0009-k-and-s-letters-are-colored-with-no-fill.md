# 0009: K and S letters are colored, with no fill

- Date: 13 October 2026
- Status: accepted
- Text confirmed by the owner: 13 October 2026
- Diagram: none
- Changes: decision 0008, two of its rules

## Question

Decision 0008 gives the K or S of each label, such as "1.1-K2", a small solid fill with a navy letter. Should the letter have only its color instead?

## Options

1. Keep the fills in both modes (decision 0008).
2. Fills in light mode, colored letters in dark mode.
3. Colored letters with no fill in both modes.

## Decision

Option 3. In both modes, the K of a label is light blue `#8BC4D9` and the S is gold `#F3A83C`, with no fill. The legend tags "K = Knowledge of" and "S = Skills in" keep their solid fills.

This changes two rules of decision 0008:

- "Gold is never a text color" now has one exception: the S of a label.
- "The K or S of each label has the same solid fill with a navy letter" is replaced by the decision above.

## Reason

The owner said that the letter in its color is enough, with no fill behind it.

## How we decided

The assistant showed that the kit colors are pale as text on white: about 1.8 to 1 for light blue and 2.0 to 1 for gold, below the 4.5 to 1 rule for small text. In dark mode they are 9.5 to 1 and 7.4 to 1. The assistant recommended option 2. The owner looked at screenshots of option 2 and option 3 and chose option 3.

## Known limits

- In light mode, the K and S letters are hard to read. A reader sees the color, and can read the letter in the legend and in the guide's own list headings "Knowledge of" and "Skills in".
