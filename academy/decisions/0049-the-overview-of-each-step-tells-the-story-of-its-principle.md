## Question

Each closed step of tab 2 shows a principle (decision 0046). When a reader opens a step, the Overview still described what the step does, in the tone of a manual. How does the Overview connect to the principle?

## Decision

1. Each of the 9 Overviews has three beats: the risk, what we built, and the result.
2. It is in the story tone of tab 2 and within 100 words.
3. Each sentence that states a fact opens its card.
4. An independent agent checks each sentence against its card.

## Reason

The principle on the heading says why the step exists. The Overview must show that reason in 30 seconds, with one result from the records, so that a learner sees the exam idea at work and a leader sees the judgment behind it.

## How we decided

The assistant recommended step 0 as a sample. The owner saw it and asked to see step 1 first. The assistant showed the old and the new text of step 1. The owner then said: "You know what you don't need my approval just do the changes for all the steps. But pay close attention to preview cards". The independent check found 15 places where a sentence said more than its card. The assistant fixed all of them, and the page kept the same 244 cards.
