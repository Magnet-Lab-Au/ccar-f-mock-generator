## Question

Decision 0039, item 12, says: "Section 17 of the guide is called "the list of topics that the exam does not test"." The assistant recommended this name, and the owner accepted it. The name is wrong. In `reference/exam-guide.md`, section 17 is "Appendix" (line 993). It has three lists: "Technologies and Concepts", "In-Scope Topics" and "Out-of-Scope Topics". "The exam tests" on tab 1 quotes the first two lists. What name does tab 1 use?

## Decision

Tab 1 calls section 17 "the appendix of the guide". "How we broke it down" says once that "The exam tests" quotes the appendix of the guide (its section 17), and that the appendix lists the technologies and concepts, the topics that the exam covers, and the topics that it does not cover. The cards say "Exam guide, appendix (section 17)".

## Reason

The name of decision 0039 would tell a candidate that "The exam tests" quotes topics that the exam does not test. That is the opposite of the truth.

## How we decided

The assistant found the error when it made the change on tab 1. It read the headings of section 17 in the guide, used the correct name, and wrote this file.
