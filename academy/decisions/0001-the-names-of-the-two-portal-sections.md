# 0001: The names of the two portal sections

- Date: 12 October 2026
- Status: accepted
- Diagram: none

## Question

The MVP brief (`academy/brief.md`) has two sections. It calls them "Exam guide breakdown" and "Case study". What names do the sections get in the portal?

## Options

1. "Exam guide breakdown" and "Case study", as in the brief.
2. "Exam guide breakdown" and "Behind the mocks".

## Decision

Option 2. The first section is "Exam guide breakdown". The second section is "Behind the mocks". An entry of the second section is a "Behind the mocks" entry.

## Reason

Plain reason: the owner did not know what "case study entry" meant. The name "Behind the mocks" tells the participant what the section shows.

## How we decided

The assistant explained the two sections of the brief. The owner then named the sections.
