# 0006: The label "Beyond the exam"

- Date: 13 October 2026
- Status: accepted
- Diagram: none

## Question

Some stories of the build teach more than the exam tests. Prompt caching is the example. How does a page show that?

## Options

1. No label.
2. A small label "Beyond the exam" on each such part, with one line that tells what the exam tests.

## Decision

Option 2. Example: on the prompt caching story, the label "Beyond the exam" and the line "The exam tests only that prompt caching exists."

## Reason

Section 17 of the exam guide lists, under "Out-of-Scope Topics", "Prompt caching implementation details (beyond knowing it exists)" and "Rate limiting, quotas, or API pricing calculations". Without a label, a participant can study details that the exam does not ask about. These readers have little time.

## How we decided

The owner said that prompt caching is part of the syllabus. The assistant checked Section 17 of the guide and showed that only its existence is in scope. The assistant suggested the label. The owner agreed.

## Exam guide points

none. The reason uses Section 17, "Out-of-Scope Topics".
