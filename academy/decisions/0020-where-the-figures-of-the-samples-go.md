## Question

The two 1.1 samples (`sample/1-1-split-exam-guide.html` and `sample/1-1-split-behind-the-mocks.html`) show one task statement. In the outline of decision 0010, some of their figures belong in other places. Where does each figure go?

## Decision

| Figure | Now in | Moves to |
|---|---|---|
| The domain weights and the 6 scenarios (Fig. 3 of the tab 1 sample) | tab 1, inside 1.1 | the map at the top of tab 1 |
| The loop, the anti-patterns and the stop reasons (Fig. 1, 2 and 4 of the tab 1 sample) | tab 1, inside 1.1 | stay in 1.1 |
| "A pass stops the loop" and "83 tries for 60 slots" (Fig. 1 and 3 of the tab 2 sample) | tab 2 sample | step 5, Explanation |
| "Code makes a plan of 60 slots" (Fig. 2 of the tab 2 sample) | tab 2 sample | step 1, Explanation |
| The review loop that became code (Fig. 4 of the tab 2 sample) | tab 2 sample | step 5, Depth |

## Reason

The domain weights and the scenarios are the same for all 30 task statements, so they go once on the map. Each figure of the tab 2 sample shows one step of the builder, so it goes into that step (decision 0010).

## How we decided

The independent review of the plan found that the samples do not fit the new outline. The assistant recommended the table. The owner accepted it.
