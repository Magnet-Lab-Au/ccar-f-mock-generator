# D1: the figures without their captions

`academy/build/shot-figure.js` got the mode `nocap`, which hides the caption, the step captions, the panel captions and the sentence of a live figure. Two agents saw only these pictures (56 figures) and wrote the main claim of each. The assistant compared each claim with the caption and judged it: match, partial or miss. A review agent checked 24 rows of this table and changed 5 judgments (see the tab 2 heading). It found 3.1-1 and 3.5-1 of tab 1 borderline.

The panel headings of before/after figures and the headings of small bar charts stayed visible (for example "Before: near-identical descriptions"). They are labels of the drawing, so for those figures the test did not fully separate the drawing from its words.
