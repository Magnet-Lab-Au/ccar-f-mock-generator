It counts the sentences of the page text, without code, the guide's own point texts, quotes and the cards.

## The agent

It played a capable developer who reads technical English slowly and uses a translation tool. It read 6 parts of tab 1, 5 parts of tab 2, and the list of the script.

Verdict: the pages are mostly simple enough. The barriers are smaller: one word with two meanings ("second review", "hold", "start", "point"), pronouns whose reference is in the sentence before, some idioms ("near case", "blind", "get the most out of", "whole-picture"), a few compressed sentences in the Depth of tab 2 (mainly step 5), and IDs that the page does not explain.

Fixed: "second review" in step 5 of tab 2 now says "second design review" where it means the design review (see `changes.md`).

For the owner (rewrites of text), most harmful first:

1. "How we broke it down": the sentence that explains the label "Guide only" is hard to read ("ends a point", "copied page").
2. Step 5: "the branch for a failed try of the start runs before the fix, and the test checks only the first try after the decision" needed three reads.
3. Step 5: "the slot is open" (open can mean free or not finished), and "2 results 'cannot decide' of step 4 also stop it".
4. "hold" means "be true" in step 1 and "contain" in step 7.
5. "blind" (4.6 and step 7) is an idiom.

8. "a near case" (2.2 and 5.2), "repairs a rate limit", "cuts an error text".

17. The top paragraph of tab 2 is not in Simplified Technical English ("in action", "traces those principles back", British "recognise"). The owner wrote this paragraph (journal, 13 October 2026), so the assistant does not change it.
