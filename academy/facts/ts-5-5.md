## 2. The takeaway

**Proposed heading (8 words):** "Trust confidence scores only after labeled data calibrates them"

**Short answer:** One total accuracy can hide a weak document type or field, so measure each segment before you cut human review. Route low-confidence or contradictory extractions to people, with thresholds set on labeled data, and keep a random sample of the confident ones under review.

Support: F1 to F3 (K1, K4, S2), F4, F5 (K2, S1), F6 to F9 (K3, S3), F10 to F12 (S4).

## 5. Beyond the exam

1. **Human grading is slow, so spend it well.** DT 2841: "2. **Human grading:** Most flexible and high quality, but slow and expensive. Avoid if possible."
