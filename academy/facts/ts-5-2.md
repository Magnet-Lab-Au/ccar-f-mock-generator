## 2. The takeaway

**Proposed heading (9 words):** "Escalate on explicit criteria, not on mood or confidence"

**Short answer:** Write the escalation criteria and a few examples into the system prompt: a request for a human, a gap in the policy, and no progress. Do not escalate on sentiment or on a confidence score that the model gives itself, and ask for one more identifier when a lookup finds several customers.

Support: F1, F2 (K1), F3 to F7 (S1), F8 (K2, S2, S3), F10, F11 (K3), F14 to F18 (K4, S5). The documentation supports S1 and S5 only. The rest rests on the guide (section 10).
