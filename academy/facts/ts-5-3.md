## 2. The takeaway

**Proposed heading (9 words):** "A failed subagent reports what failed, never a silent empty"

**Short answer:** A subagent retries a transient failure itself. If it cannot fix the failure, it tells the coordinator the failure type, the query, the partial results and the alternatives, so that the coordinator can retry, change the plan or continue with a marked gap.
