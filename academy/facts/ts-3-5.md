## 2. The takeaway

**Proposed heading (9 words):** "Refine with examples, failing tests, interviews and grouped fixes"

**Short answer:** When prose gives mixed results, show Claude 2 or 3 input and output examples, or write the tests first and let the failures drive the next try. Before work in an unfamiliar domain, let Claude interview you, and send interacting issues in one message but independent issues one at a time.

Support: F1 to F3 (examples, C1, C2), F4 to F9 (tests first), F10 to F13 (interview, C3), F14 (one message or a sequence, C4).
