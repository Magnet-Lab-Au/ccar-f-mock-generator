The exam guide says

    Why here

    The path of a question that fails

    • A question can fail at step 3 or step 4. The checker of step 4 can also say "cannot decide". Step 6 sends no question back: each of its findings waits for the owner, who selects "ignore" or "rewrite". The owner can also mark a question bad in step 7.
    • In each case it goes to step 5. The writer gets the question and the exact findings, and writes it again. The new question goes through the checks again.
    • The number of tries has a limit. After the limit, the slot stays open and the owner decides.
    • Too many bad marks of the owner stop the mock. The rules of the checker are corrected, and all 60 questions are checked again.
