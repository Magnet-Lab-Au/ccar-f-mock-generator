## Question

How many mocks does the program make? Decisions 0027 and 0047 left this open. It decides how the program selects the scenarios across mocks, and if the rule of the point groups also applies across mocks.

## Options

None. The owner gave the number.

## Decision

The program makes 5 mocks.

## Reason

Plain reason: the portal needs 5 mocks.

What the number means for the point groups (decision 0047):

- 5 mocks have 300 slots. The guide has 95 groups (ideas). So each idea comes in about 3 of the 5 mocks.
- A rule "no idea in two mocks" is not possible.
- Over 200 seeds, two plans share about 44 of their 60 ideas (36 to 51). 12 ideas are in each plan.

Not decided yet:

- How the program spreads the ideas and the scenarios over the 5 mocks.
- How the program prevents the same scene in two mocks.

## How we decided

The assistant measured how many ideas two mocks share, and said that the limit across mocks depends on the number of mocks. The owner said: "We need 5 mocks."
