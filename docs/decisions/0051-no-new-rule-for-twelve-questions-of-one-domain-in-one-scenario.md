# 0051: No new rule for 12 questions of one domain in one scenario

- Date: 8 October 2026
- Status: accepted
- Text confirmed by the owner: 8 October 2026
- Diagram: none

## Question

Change 3 of decision 0046: "Scenario 5 has 12 of its 15 questions from Domain 4 (the scenario limit of decision 0027)." Does step 1 need a rule against this?

## Options

1. No new rule. The point groups (decisions 0047 and 0048) already prevent the harm.
2. A limit on the questions of one domain in one scenario.

## Decision

Option 1. Step 1 does not change.

## Reason

The cause is in the guide. Only scenarios 5 and 6 have Domain 4 as a primary domain (Section 5 of the guide). Decision 0027 says that each question is in a primary domain of its scenario. So when a set of 4 scenarios has only one of scenarios 5 and 6, that scenario gets all 12 questions of Domain 4. This happens in 9 of the 11 sets.

A limit does not work. A script checked each limit below 12 questions of one domain in one scenario:

| Limit | Sets of scenarios left | Scenario that is never used |
|---|---|---|
| 11 or 10 | 2 of 11: 1-4-5-6 and 3-4-5-6 | Scenario 2 |
| 9 or less | 0 of 11 | All |

The harm in mock 1 was a repeat: M1-S53 and M1-S54 had the same scene (decision 0046). The points 4.4-K1 and 4.4-K4 are now in one group, so no mock gets both. A scenario with 12 questions of Domain 4 still gets 12 different ideas.

## How we decided

The assistant checked the tables of all 11 sets and each limit. It suggested no new rule. The owner said yes.

No independent reviewer checked this file.
