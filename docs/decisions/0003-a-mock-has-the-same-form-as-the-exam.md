# 0003: A mock has the same form as the exam

- Date: 6 October 2026
- Status: accepted
- Text confirmed by the owner: 6 October 2026
- Diagram: none

## Question

What form does a finished mock have?

## Options

1. The same form as the exam.
2. The exam form as the base, with changes.
3. A different form.

## Decision

Option 1. A mock has the form that the exam guide gives in Sections 3 and 4:

- 60 questions and 120 minutes.
- 4 scenarios from the 6 scenarios of the guide.
- Multiple-choice and multiple-response questions. Each question says how many answers to select.
- The approximate domain weights 27%, 18%, 20%, 20% and 15%.

## Reason

The form comes from the exam guide: Section 3 (the exam details) and Section 4 (the domain weights). A participant uses a mock to practise the real conditions. A mock with a different form trains the wrong pace.

Not decided yet: how many questions are multiple-response, how many questions each scenario has, and the exact number of questions for each domain. The guide does not give the first two numbers, and it gives the weights as approximate.

## How we decided

The assistant suggested this. We decided the end of the pipeline before its steps, because we cannot design steps toward an output that is not defined.

## Exam guide points

none. The decision uses Sections 3 and 4 of the guide. They are not task statement points.
