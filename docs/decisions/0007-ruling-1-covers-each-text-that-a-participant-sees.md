# 0007: Ruling 1 covers each text that a participant sees

## Question

Decision 0005 says that no question depends on a feature that the exam guide does not name. Does the rule also cover the explanation in the answer key?

## Options

1. Yes. The rule covers each text that a participant sees.
2. No. The rule covers the question and its options only.
3. A different limit.

## Decision

Option 1. This decision adds to decision 0005. It does not replace it. The rule covers each text that a participant sees: the question, its options and the explanation in the answer key.

Two details follow from the text of 0005 and need no decision:

- A flag that the guide itself gives as a wrong option is a named feature. It is a fair wrong option. Example: `--batch` and `CLAUDE_HEADLESS` in sample question 10.
- Where the guide has its own word for a thing, the text uses that word. Example: "iteration caps" (task statement 1.1), not "maxTurns".

## Reason

This decision completes ruling 1 of the owner. A participant learns most from the explanation. An explanation that uses a feature that the guide does not name teaches content that the exam does not test. The two details in the Decision use Section 9 (sample question 10) and task statement 1.1 of the guide.

Not decided yet: the format of the answer key, and which texts it has.

## How we decided

The original brief of the ruling (`REVIEW3.md` in `CCAR-F Mocks v2/reference/first-build/rubric/`) says: "No option, explanation or trap may rely on" such a feature. Decision 0005 used a summary that did not have this detail.

none. The decision uses Sections 9 and 17 of the guide.
