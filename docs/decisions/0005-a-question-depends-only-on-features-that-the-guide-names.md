# 0005: A question depends only on features that the exam guide names

- Date: 6 October 2026
- Status: accepted
- Text confirmed by the owner: 6 October 2026
- Diagram: none

## Question

Does ruling 1 of the owner, of 29 September 2026, apply to the new build?

## Options

1. Yes, it applies.
2. Yes, with a change.
3. No.

## Decision

Option 1. No question depends on a feature that the exam guide does not name. "The exam guide" is the complete guide: the scenarios (Section 5), the task statements (Section 6), the sample questions (Section 9) and the appendix (Section 17).

## Reason

This is ruling 1 of the owner, of 29 September 2026. The owner confirmed on 6 October 2026 that it applies.

The guide supports it. Section 17 lists the technologies that "might appear on the exam", the topics that are "explicitly tested" and the topics that "will not appear". A question about a feature that the guide does not name trains a participant for something that the exam does not test.

Not decided yet: how the program checks the rule, and what counts as a "feature" and as "named".

## How we decided

The assistant asked about the ruling before the design of the first step, so that the ruling exists before the build.
